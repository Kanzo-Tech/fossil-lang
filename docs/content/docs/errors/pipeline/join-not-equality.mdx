---
title: A join condition that is not an equality
description: "`pipeline/join-not-equality` — a `join`'s `on` is not an equality, or a conjunction of equalities, between its two sides."
---

`on` is taken apart on `and`, and every piece has to be an `==`. A comparison, a disjunction or a
constant is not:

```fossil
Overlap := Booking.join(Room, on = Booking.since < Room.until)
Either  := Left.join(Right, on = Left.k == Right.k or Left.id == Right.rid)
```

```text
`join` in `Overlap` relates its two sides by equality, and `Booking.since < Room.until` is not one
```

The condition quoted is the piece that failed, not the whole `on` — for the `or` it is the whole
disjunction, because only `and` splits a condition.

## Why fossil refuses it

These are ordinary relational conditions, and an algebra would allow them. fossil refuses them for
the engine's reason: a theta join or a disjunction leaves no equality to plan a hash join against,
so what would execute is a nested loop over the product of the two relations — and a corpus is the
size at which that is not a slow program but a hung one.

The rule is older than the check. The engine's planner has refused such a condition since joins
first executed, but only at `fossil run`, with no span, because by then the program is a plan. The
checker refuses it at `fossil check`, over the text you wrote. [The
algebra](/docs/design/algebra#the-join-condition-is-a-predicate-not-a-key) covers the two questions a
condition has to answer, and [relations](/docs/book/relations#join) the conditions that are allowed.

## How to repair it

The `help` states the form: an equality between one column of each side, `on = A.k == B.k`, or
several joined by `and`.

- **A compound key.** Join the equalities with `and`:
  `on = Left.k == Right.k and Left.id == Right.rid`.
- **A range or an inequality alongside a real key.** Join on the equality the two relations do
  share, and put the comparison in a `where` after the join, where both sides' columns are in
  scope.
- **No shared key at all.** Then there is nothing to join on inside fossil; a key has to be
  prepared in the source.
