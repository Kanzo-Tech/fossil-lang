title: Where x and y come from
description: "The corpus carries the graph, not a picture of it. Where a vertex is drawn is the view's choice: two numeric columns it binds, or its own GPU force simulation."
---

A corpus has no `x`, no `y`, no community column and no `position` in `fossil.json`. It carries the
vertices, the edges and the program's columns, numbered so that a view can upload them as they are:
`dense_id` is `0..V−1` with each type one contiguous range, and an edge's `src`/`dst` are those ids
([identity](/docs/format/conventions/identity)).

**Where a vertex is drawn is the view's choice**, and it has two:

- **two numeric columns it binds** — `lon` and `lat`, or any pair the program wrote. A vertex table
  without both is not drawn under that binding; the corpus does not invent a position for it.
- **its own force simulation on the GPU**, when nothing is bound — seeded, settled in the tab, over
  exactly the vertices and edges the corpus holds.

That is Cosmograph's architecture — DuckDB-WASM, a Mosaic coordinator, cosmos.gl drawing points by
index ([prior art](/docs/design/prior-art#where-a-vertex-is-drawn)) — and fossil's writer is
the half of it that numbers points and links.

## Why the writer stopped placing

A placement computed by the writer is computed before anyone has chosen a view, and every one tried
drew something the view then had to undo. Nine were built and none drew a readable graph;
[what was discarded](/docs/design/discarded#the-corpus) lists them. The last ran Louvain in the
user's tab for 113–127 s of a 159 s write at 10M vertices.

## The measurement that decided it

The gate was the view's simulation, on the hardware a reader has: cosmos.gl 3.4.0, Apple M4 Pro,
Chrome 154 headed, real GPU.

| N vertices | settings | settles in | frame |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 200k | links hidden during the simulation, `simulationDecay` ≈ 600 | ~10–12 s | ~17 ms |
| 500k | `simulationDecay` ≈ 240, `spaceSize` 8192, links off | ~11 s | ~48 ms |
| ~50k | links drawn throughout | the practical limit | |

The layout's shape is there within 1–3 s at every N. Loading the buffers (`setPointPositions` +
`setLinks`) is synchronous: 89 ms at 10k, 229 ms at 50k, ~450 ms at 200k, ~1,100 ms at 500k.

**What would reverse it.** A measured corpus the GPU simulation cannot lay out — above the N this
table reaches — that a view must still draw. Then a placement comes back, for that size, and
[what was discarded](/docs/design/discarded#the-corpus) says which of the nine not to repeat.
