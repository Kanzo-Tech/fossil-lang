> **Planned, not started.** A program cannot put a property on an edge today, and the relation is a
> vertex instead (below). What starts it: SHACL 1.2 — `sh:reifierShape` — and Turtle 1.2 reaching
> W3C Recommendation. Both were Working Drafts on 2026-10-09 (SHACL 1.2 Core, 28 August; Turtle 1.2,
> 3 September).

## Why it waits

A property's name and type are read from the shape: a bare key is the last segment of a predicate the
shape declares, and a key no predicate declares is an error ([inference from shapes](/docs/design/shapes)).
An edge is a triple, and a property of a triple is a statement about a statement — a **reifier**. RDF
1.1 has no term for it; RDF 1.2 does, and SHACL 1.2's `sh:reifierShape` is the only way a shape
language declares what a reifier carries. ShEx has no counterpart.

Writing the columns before then needs one of two things, and both are refused: a checker that accepts
an edge property no shape declares, which is the guarantee the language exists for, or an extension of
ShEx or SHACL that no other tool reads.

## What a program writes until then

The relation as a vertex — the W3C's [n-ary relations](https://www.w3.org/TR/swbp-n-aryRelations/)
pattern, schema.org's `Role`, Wikidata's qualified statement. RDF 1.1, any shape language, no change to
the compiler:

```
Awards : Award from PrizeRow
    @subject = "https://example.org/award/{PrizeRow.id}"
    year     = parse.year(PrizeRow.awardYear)
    laureate = Laureate(PrizeRow.laureate)
    category = Category(PrizeRow.category)
```

A reader filtering by `year` greys the awards of other years and every edge into them, and the
laureates — which have no `year` — stay in full colour.

## The design, when it starts

- **Surface.** The annotation the grammar retired comes back on an edge-valued property, in Turtle
  1.2's own shape (`:a :p :b {| :q v |}`) and GQL's: `won = Category(Row.category) { year = parse.year(Row.year) }`.
  The `ANNOTATION_BLOCK` tombstone in `grammar.bnf` is where it returns.
- **Types.** The edge's predicate carries `sh:reifierShape`; the annotation's keys resolve against that
  shape's predicates exactly as a mapping body's resolve against its target's. ShEx programs cannot
  annotate until ShEx can say the same thing.
- **Corpus.** The properties are columns of the relation's edge table after `src` and `dst`, with
  `iri` and `datatype` as on a vertex table, and `fossil/1` grows by fields a reader may ignore.
  `finalize_edge` takes `distinct` over every column, not the pair: two dates on one pair are two edges.
- **Readers.** A link gets an index as a vertex does — its table's first row plus its row in
  `(src, dst)` order — so a viewer greys a link by index. A clause over an edge column keeps the edges
  it matches and their endpoints, Cosmograph's `useLinksData` rule.
- **RDF.** The view writes one reifier per annotated edge (`rdf:reifies`) under Turtle 1.2.

## What would reverse it

A shape language this compiler reads that declares edge properties without reification — a
property-graph schema (PG-Schema, GQL's graph types) becoming a third decoder beside ShEx and SHACL.
Then the annotation is typed from there, and it need not wait for SHACL 1.2.
